Oppenheimer

Overview

A dramatization of the life story of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the physicist who had a large hand in the development of the atomic bomb, thus helping end World War II. We see his life from university days all the way to post-WWII, where his loyalty to the United States is questioned and he is brought before a review board.
